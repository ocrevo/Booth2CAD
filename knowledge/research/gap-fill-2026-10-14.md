# Gap-fill research, 2026-10-14

- Scope: six documented gaps in `dimensions-reference.md`. Access date for every URL: **2026-10-14**.
- Units: mm unless a row says otherwise. Anything marked *(derived)* is my arithmetic, not a source value.
- Confidence: **high** = manufacturer, organiser or standards-platform page read directly. **medium** = third-party copy or a search-engine extract of a page I did not open. **low** = dealer or single listing, or text I could only see as a fragment.
- Source tags used in the tables:
  - **[OFFICIAL]** = text read on the maker's, organiser's, venue's or government site.
  - **[3RD]** = text read on a third-party copy (dealer, forum, standards reseller, vendor blog).
  - **[EXTRACT]** = I saw the content only inside a search-engine result extract and did not open the page or file.
- Quotes are short excerpts as returned by the page-fetch tool, which summarises pages. They were not re-read in a browser.
- Method deviations (please read):
  - The fetch tool saved **4 PDFs** to its own cache outside the project, without my asking. Three are Prolyte data sheets (`...52se5k.pdf`, `...d7kzam.pdf`, `...hce6v4.pdf`) and one is a gov.cn PDF (`...76aai1.pdf`), all in the fetch tool's own cache folder under the user profile. The text in them was unreadable (compressed streams), so nothing in this file comes from them. After the 3 data sheets I avoided PDFs; the gov.cn one was fetched by mistake. PDFs below are therefore cited from search extracts only. I did not delete the cached files; delete them if you want them gone.
  - Search extracts are AI-written summaries. One of them (the "orchestra.media GITEX 2026 exhibitor guide") described content that the page itself did not contain. I dropped it. Treat every [EXTRACT] row as a lead to confirm.
