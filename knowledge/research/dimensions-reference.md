# Dimension reference for booth reconstruction (scale anchors and defaults)

> **Unreviewed reference notes.** Collected from public sources on the access date given below and not reviewed by a booth designer. They are not defaults of the tool, not facts about any client project and not legal advice; check the linked source before relying on a value.

- Purpose: give the team cited sizes for reconstructing exhibition stands from single photos or renders. The sizes are for anchoring image scale and for defaults when a brief gives none.
- Access date for every URL: **2026-10-14**, unless a row says otherwise.
- Units: mm, unless a row says otherwise. Inch values were converted at 25.4 mm/in and rounded to 1 mm. Conversions and derived numbers are marked *(derived)*.
- Quotes are short excerpts from the cited source.
- Confidence scale:
  - **High**: manufacturer spec sheet, official standard text, or organiser or contractor technical document, read directly.
  - **Medium**: authorised dealer, reputable secondary reference, third-party reproduction of a standard, or an official page seen only through a search-engine extract because the page blocked direct fetching.
  - **Low**: vendor blog or single non-authoritative listing.
- Rule: no number here is unsourced. Where a source could not be verified, the item is listed under **Gaps** at the end, not given a number.

## 0. Corrections to the brief's working assumptions

| Assumption in brief | What the sources say |
|---|---|
| "~1000 mm" module | Octanorm and shell-scheme systems run on a **990 mm axis**: a 950 mm seen panel plus a 40 mm upright. A "3 m" row booth is 3 × 990 = 2970 mm on axis. beMatrix uses **992 mm** (16 × 62 mm). The only "1000 mm" walls found were 1000 × 2500 panels, in a Macau organiser's rental list and in Chinese suppliers' "1 × 2.5 m" panel listings. |
| ADA "28–34 in / 711–864 mm" for counters | ADA **902.3** gives 28–34 in (710–865 mm) for **dining and work surfaces**. Sales and service **counters** fall under **904.4**: an accessible section **≤ 36 in (915 mm)** high. |
| Eye height 1600 mm | Plausible. In DIN 33402-2 data, standing eye height runs from 1430 mm (women P5) to 1735 mm (men P95), so 1600 mm lies inside that range. One rendering vendor recommends 1.5–1.8 m. |
| Platforms 50 / 100 mm | Partly supported. Confirmed platform heights are 50 mm (Koelnmesse modular stand), 40 mm (Messe Stuttgart), 43 mm (Octanorm OCTAeco) and 100 mm (OCTAeco panel plus one adapter). Organiser rules change at 50, 100 and 120 mm. |

### 2a. 16:9 flat panels. Formula and manufacturer data

**Formula (16:9 active area from diagonal d in inches):**
W = d · 25.4 · 16/√337 = **22.138 · d** mm. H = d · 25.4 · 9/√337 = **12.453 · d** mm.
Outline = active area + 2 × border (bezel plus mask) on each axis. Use the *measured* diagonal where the maker gives one: "class" sizes are rounded up (a 43" class panel measures 42.5").

### 6c. Suggested defaults when the brief gives nothing (each lies inside a sourced range above)

- Wall module **990** axis with **950** seen panel, height **2500** overall. If the frames are flush beMatrix-style, use **992 × 2480**.
- Panel thickness **3** for shell scheme and beMatrix infills. **2** for OCTAwall fabric frames.
- Platform **50**, ramp **1:12**.
- Bar table **Ø 800 × 1100** (EU) or **Ø 762 × 1067** (US). Bistro table **Ø 700 × 760**. Meeting table height **720–750**.
- Counter top **1000** for a system counter (sourced range 900–1150). Accessible section ≤ 865 (work surface) or ≤ 915 (service counter).
- Eye height for renders **1600** (inside the DIN 33402-2 P5–P95 range).
- Screen: decide the size class from the image. Then use the maker active area. If you cannot decide, flag the image instead of guessing.

## Gaps (not verified, so no numbers are given)

- **EN 527-1:2011** fixed desk heights: only the standard's scope was reachable. The height table is paywalled.
- **GB/T 9846-2015** plywood size table: not retrieved. 1220 × 2440 here comes from an EPD and trade practice, not the GB text.
- **BS 8300-2:2018** counter heights (section 16, figure 21): preview only.
- **GB 55019-2021** (newer Chinese accessibility code): not checked against GB 50763-2012.
- **Octanorm "1000 mm grid"**: not found. All Octanorm sources show a 990 axis.
- **beMatrix full height list**: the official frame pages returned HTTP 500. Heights come from product codes plus a third-party list.
- **Chinese system**: no formal section drawing or spec sheet. The values come from manufacturers' web pages. The flat-beam sizes "4分/5分/7分" were not converted, because no source defines them in mm.
- **Vitra.com, Absen, Hikvision, Unilumin, Sixteen:Nine, Adexpo, EFG** blocked direct fetching. Rows from these sources are marked Medium and rely on search extracts or third-party copies.
- **Eames replicas** and generic "Tulip" copies are not covered.
- **Messe Essen platform and stair sheet** (riser ≤ 190, tread ≥ 260 per a search extract): the download link was dead, so the figures are excluded.
