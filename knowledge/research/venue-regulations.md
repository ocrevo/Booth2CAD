# Venue and organiser rules for custom-built (space-only) stands

- **Purpose:** collect the hard constraints that venues and organisers publish for custom-built stands, so `rebuild-booth-blender` can pick defensible defaults when the client gives no data.
- **Access date for every URL below: 2026-10-14.**
- **Method:** official PDFs were downloaded and read as full text, and web pages were fetched. Where a venue's own regulations were not online, an organiser's exhibitor manual for an event at that venue is used. Those rows are labelled with the event, because they are **event rules, not venue rules**. Quotes are kept under 25 words; everything else is paraphrased. Values are converted to mm, with the original unit in brackets where it was not metric.
- **Every value in this note is venue-specific, and usually edition- and event-specific.** Organisers can set stricter or looser limits than the venue at the same hall (example: NECC Shanghai allows 6,000 mm at CIIE 2025 but 4,000 mm at CBME 2025). Always re-check the manual for the actual show.
- **Confidence:** **high** = read in the full official document on the venue's or organiser's own domain. **medium** = read in the full document but hosted by a third party, *or* an organiser manual standing in for venue rules, *or* the table was partly garbled in extraction. **low** = only seen in a summary or listing, not in the full text.
- Abbreviations: MF Messe Frankfurt, MD Messe Düsseldorf, MM Messe München, KM Koelnmesse, DM Deutsche Messe (Hannover), NECC National Exhibition and Convention Center (Shanghai), CF Canton Fair Complex, SZW Shenzhen World, CIEC China International Exhibition Center (Shunyi), DWTC Dubai World Trade Centre, LVCC Las Vegas Convention Center.

## 1. Key findings

1. **The most consistent rule is the neutral back wall above 2,500 mm.** MF, MD, MM, KM and DM all require walls facing a neighbour to be neutral (white, grey or beige; smooth or opaque) above 2,500 mm. Intersec Global (DWTC) and the 2026 Shenzhen toy fair do too. Chinese manuals (CIIE, Canton Fair, CIEC events) require exposed backs to be finished with **no advertising at any height**. LVCC (CONEXPO 2026) requires a plain finished back wall with no graphics facing the neighbour.
2. **Single-storey height limits run from 2,000 to 8,000 mm and depend on hall, stand size and event.** Germany: MF gives 4,000 mm for stands up to 30 m² in most halls and up to 8,000 mm for larger stands. MD ranges 2,000–8,000 mm by hall, DM is 6,500 mm, and KM's interzum page gives 4,500 mm. China: 4,000–6,000 mm (Canton Fair 4,500, CIEC events 5,000, NECC 4,000 or 6,000 depending on organiser, Shenzhen World events 3,500–5,000).
3. **Approval triggers cluster around 100 m² and 3–4.5 m.** MF needs approval above 100 m² or above 4 m. MM needs it above 100 m² or above 3 m, and DM above 100 m². CIIE sends stands of 4.5 m or more to an appointed structural reviewer. Every venue needs approval for double-decks.
4. **Fire class.** China: build and decoration materials and carpets at least **B1 (难燃)** under the GB 8624 scheme. The Canton Fair adds class **A** for ceilings and double-deck floor slabs. Germany: **DIN 4102 B1** or EN 13501-1 equivalents. MF, KM, MM and DM name C-s2,d0, MD accepts C-s3,d0, and MF names C-s2,d2 for general materials with C-s2,d0 overhead and Cfl-s1 for floors. DWTC requires drapes to meet **NFPA 701**.
5. **Closed ceilings are restricted.** In Germany about **30 m²** is allowed without extra sprinklers (MF, MD, MM). In China, CIIE allows covered area of 160 m² or less on single-storey stands, the Canton Fair does not allow single-storey stands to be covered, and Shenzhen events cap covered area at 50% of the booth. The **upper deck of a double-deck must stay open** at most venues.
6. **Rigging is installed by the venue or its contractor almost everywhere.** Point loads run from **50 kg** (MD; CIEC at CIMES 2024; manual hoists in some MF halls) through 100 kg (MM; other MF halls) and 150 kg (NECC at CIIE 2025) to **1 t** (Shenzhen World).
7. **Chinese standards exist, but I could not read their contents.**
   - **SB/T 11162-2016 《展台分类及技术规范》** is listed as 现行 (current) on the national standards platform.
   - **GB/T 33490-2025 《展览展示工程服务基本要求》** replaced the 2017 edition.
   - **T/CAWS 0002-2022 《会展业安全管理规范》** is a group standard.
   - None of the full texts were read (paywalled), so I cannot say whether they set height limits.
   - **GB 8624-2025** has been published and replaces GB 8624-2012 from 2027-01-01, per a third-party listing. Whether it keeps the "B1" class name is unverified.
